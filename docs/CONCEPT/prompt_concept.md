# Prompt di concept (Nano Banana)

Immagini di atmosfera e key art per fissare lo stile prima della produzione (D-36b). Non diventano texture: servono a Vlad e a Claude come **bersaglio visivo** e per i confronti nei render di revisione. Consegna: `assets_in/concept/CON-NN_v1.png` (v2, v3 per le revisioni). Claude controlla ogni immagine con la colonna «Cosa controllo» e propone correzioni con prompt di modifica.

Regole comuni (già incluse in ogni prompt): niente marchi o scritte reali, nessuna persona reale, realismo atmosferico (ART_BIBLE §1), luce del mese e dell'ora giuste (ART_BIBLE §2).

---

### CON-01 — L'atrio degli arrivi nel tardo pomeriggio
- **Scopo**: bersaglio di luce e materiali per LUO-AER-ARR (vertical slice).
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9, 2752×1536 o la massima.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Cinematic realistic photograph inside the arrivals hall of a modern Sicilian airport at 5:30 pm in mid-September. Long hall with a glass façade on one side: low warm golden sunlight enters in long diagonal beams across a polished grey granite floor, reflecting. Slatted ceiling with linear skylights, round columns clad in light stone, a metal barrier in front of a closed frosted sliding door, a small café counter on the left, people waiting with cardboard signs, seen from the eye height of a 12-year-old (1.4 m), 28mm lens, slight haze of dust in the light beams. Generic signage with no readable brand names. Warm, calm, slightly nostalgic mood.»
- **Cosa controllo**: direzione della luce (da ovest, radente); riflessi sul granito; scala della sala (soffitto ~7 m); nessuna scritta leggibile; altezza camera.
- **In breve**: l'atrio dell'aeroporto con la luce dorata del pomeriggio che entra di traverso.

### CON-02 — L'arrivo della babuška
- **Scopo**: key art del capitolo 1; riferimento per il personaggio e la composizione di SCN-01-09.
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Cinematic realistic shot from the eye height of a 12-year-old girl in an airport arrivals hall at golden hour. The frosted sliding door has just opened: a luggage trolley comes out carrying three old suitcases stacked on top of each other and tied with thick hemp string; behind it walks a small 74-year-old Russian grandmother wearing a dark bottle-green wool coat in September and a cream headscarf with small red and blue flowers tied under her chin, clutching a floral cloth shopping bag under her arm, from which the worn toe of an old bark shoe peeks out. A blond woman in a white linen shirt runs towards her with open arms. Warm light, shallow depth of field on the grandmother, other travellers soft in the background, no logos.»
- **Cosa controllo**: tre valigie con lo spago; cappotto verde e fazzoletto annodato sotto il mento; borsa sotto il braccio (non sul carrello) con la punta del lapot'; proporzioni (babuška piccola, 149 cm); mamma in corsa.
- **In breve**: la babuška esce dalla porta degli arrivi col carrello e la borsa a fiori.

### CON-03 — Gli occhietti nella scarpa
- **Scopo**: definire l'unica apparizione di Fomà nel cap. 1 (D-47).
- **Proporzioni**: 3:2.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Extreme close-up, realistic, of the opening of a floral cloth shopping bag held under an old woman's arm (green wool coat sleeve visible). Inside the bag, in the darkness of an old woven bark shoe, two tiny shiny jet-black eyes catch a warm glint of airport light, barely visible, like the eyes of a small animal. Very shallow depth of field, warm golden ambient light, mysterious but gentle mood, no creature visible except the two glossy black eyes and a hint of grey fur.»
- **Cosa controllo**: occhi **neri** e lucidi con riflesso caldo (non gialli); quasi nient'altro visibile; texture di corteccia e stoffa.
- **In breve**: due occhietti neri che brillano nel buio della scarpa.

### CON-04 — L'Etna al tramonto dal finestrino
- **Scopo**: bersaglio del viaggio in auto (SCN-01-15, FOT-01-06).
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «View from the back seat of a car driving north on a Sicilian motorway at sunset in mid-September: through the side window, Mount Etna stands large on the horizon in warm backlight, a pale plume of smoke drifting slowly from the summit, the sky orange to pink to deep blue, oleanders and a guard-rail blurring in the foreground, the faint reflection of a girl's face in the window glass. In the front passenger seat an old woman's headscarf and the edge of a floral bag on her lap are visible. Realistic, warm, quiet.»
- **Cosa controllo**: profilo dell'Etna realistico; pennacchio di fumo; riflesso nel vetro; posizione: dietro la babuška (seduta davanti a destra).
- **In breve**: l'Etna che fuma al tramonto vista dal sedile dietro.

### CON-05 — La cucina di Casa Russo di notte, con il frigorifero
- **Scopo**: bersaglio della scena notturna del cap. 2 (luce del lampione, frigo, spazio dietro).
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «A small old Sicilian house kitchen at midnight, lights off, seen from the doorway at the eye height of a 12-year-old. Orange sodium streetlight comes in through the window above the sink, drawing a stripe on the old patterned cement tile floor. A white two-door fridge from the 2000s stands against the wall with a narrow dark gap behind it and a black grille glowing faintly; a wooden table with six chairs, a gas stove, a dishwasher, wall cabinets, a hand-woven bark shoe lying on the floor next to a piece of black bread and a pinch of salt on a napkin. Quiet, mysterious, warm and cold light together, realistic.»
- **Cosa controllo**: luce del lampione dal cortile; frigo con intercapedine; lapot' con pane e sale; nessun personaggio.
- **In breve**: la cucina al buio, il frigo che ronza e la scarpa per terra.

### CON-06 — Fomà sul lapot'
- **Scopo**: key art del personaggio (D-21) e bersaglio per pelo, barba, naso, camicia.
- **Proporzioni**: 4:5.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Realistic cinematic portrait of a tiny ancient Russian house spirit, 32 cm tall, standing proudly on top of an old woven light linden-bark shoe on a kitchen floor at night: an old man with an almost cat-like face, big round shiny red nose, small shiny jet-black eyes with an offended expression, wild messy grey hair, a very long grey beard reaching the floor and trailing behind him like a train, body covered in soft grey tabby fur like an old cat, small furry hands with long yellowish nails, wearing a rough brick-red linen shirt belted with a rope. Warm rim light from a candle on the floor, cool streetlight from behind, shallow depth of field, photographed like a real creature, not a cartoon.»
- **Cosa controllo**: tutte le caratteristiche del libro (02.025) e della copertina; scala (una bottiglia da 1,5 L sarebbe alta come lui); occhi neri; naso rosso; barba a strascico.
- **In breve**: Fomà in piedi sul lapot', offeso, con la barba fino a terra.

### CON-07 — Le Donne di Fora entrano dal buco della serratura
- **Scopo**: bersaglio VFX (ART_BIBLE §11).
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «A dark entrance hall of an old Sicilian house at night. From the large old keyhole of a wooden door, a luminous white mist pours out like smoke from a chimney, gathers on the cold tiled floor and rises, taking shape from the bottom up: first a long skirt made of glowing fog, then a narrow waist, then two arms with thin gold bracelets, then the head of a tall elegant woman in a wine-red 19th-century gown with her hair held high by a tortoiseshell comb and a lace fan in her hand; her figure is semi-transparent, the skirt remains fog that flows on the floor like a waterfall. Behind her, two more trails of mist are coming through the keyhole. Faint golden particles of scent in the air. Realistic, magical, gentle, not scary.»
- **Cosa controllo**: ordine della formazione (gonna → vita → braccia → testa); semitrasparenza; gonna di nebbia; luce fredda delle Donne contro il buio caldo; serratura grande.
- **In breve**: la nebbia esce dalla serratura e diventa Donna Rosalia, dal basso in alto.

### CON-08 — Le scalinate con i gerani e i gatti
- **Scopo**: bersaglio della scalinata di Casa Russo (LUO-SCA-01) e del quartiere.
- **Proporzioni**: 2:3 verticale.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «A narrow stone stairway street in Taormina in the late afternoon of September: worn limestone steps polished in the middle, pots of red geraniums on both sides, old plastered houses in faded yellow and pink with green shutters and wrought-iron balconies, a big red tabby cat sleeping exactly on the fourth step, a lemon tree peeking over a wall, a slice of blue sea visible far below between two roofs. Warm low sun, long shadows, realistic, inviting.»
- **Cosa controllo**: pietra e intonaci della libreria materiali; gatto rosso sul quarto gradino; mare tra due tetti; nessuna insegna reale.
- **In breve**: la scalinata di casa con i gerani, il gatto rosso e il mare tra i tetti.

### CON-09 — Pagine del quaderno: cellule e fotosintesi
- **Scopo**: stile del quaderno (D-41) — prima pagina.
- **Proporzioni**: 3:2 (doppia pagina A5).
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Top-down photograph of an open A5 school notebook with 5 mm square grid paper and a red margin line. Left page: title «LA CELLULA» handwritten in blue ballpoint pen and underlined twice, a careful graphite pencil drawing of a plant cell with labelled parts (parete, membrana, nucleo, cloroplasti) in blue pen and pencil arrows, a printed red teacher's mark «8». Right page: «LA FOTOSINTESI» in blue pen, a pencil sketch of a leaf with sun, arrows and simple words. Only blue ballpoint and graphite pencil, slight pen smudges, eraser marks, a 12-year-old's tidy handwriting, soft daylight.»
- **Cosa controllo**: solo biro blu e matita (il rosso solo nel voto); grafia da dodicenne; quadretti 5 mm; testo in italiano corretto (se sbagliato, Claude ridisegna i testi nelle texture).
- **In breve**: le pagine di scienze del quaderno di Vera, a biro blu e matita.

### CON-13 — La casa malata e la casa guarita (due immagini gemelle)
- **Scopo**: stati di SIS-12.
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9 (due immagini).
- **Prompt A (EN)**: «Interior of an old narrow Sicilian house staircase in late October morning light: a thin crack runs like a pencil-drawn lightning bolt from the wall at the height of the ninth step up to the ceiling, a piece of plaster fallen on the ninth step; through the open door to the tiny courtyard a lemon tree with almost bare branches like fingers and yellow curled leaves on the ground; a kitchen tap running with brownish water in the background. Desaturated, slightly greenish-grey, sad but not horror.»
- **Prompt B (EN)**: «The SAME staircase and view, same framing, on a bright clear November morning: the wall is smooth and white with no crack, the lemon tree has one single white blossom on a bare branch, the tap runs clear water, warm light. Change nothing else.» (modifica della prima immagine)
- **Cosa controllo**: stessa inquadratura; crepa al 9° gradino; un solo fiore; differenza di grading.
- **In breve**: la stessa scala con la crepa e il limone spoglio, poi guarita col fiore.

### CON-14 — Taormina nel primo temporale di novembre
- **Scopo**: bersaglio meteo del cap. 14.
- **Proporzioni**: 16:9.
- **Prompt (EN)**: «Taormina, Sicily, on a rainy November evening after the first autumn storm: wet shiny stone stairways reflecting the warm streetlights, cats sheltering under old wooden doorways with offended faces, low grey clouds racing over the roofs, light rain, the smell of wet earth suggested by the colours, the main pedestrian street almost empty of tourists, a café with locals inside.
